📋 Task Management Tips

  • Priority: Start with LOW for easier tasks, then the system auto-escalates to MEDIUM and HIGH as due time gets closer
  • Due Date: Define when the task must be completed
  • Reminder: Get notified before the due date
  • Status: Tasks automatically switch to OVERDUE after the due time is crossed
  • Description: Add detailed notes to keep context
